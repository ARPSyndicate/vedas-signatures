# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, critique signatures rather than people, and do not harass anyone. Detection engineering involves disagreement about trade-offs. Back your view with evidence such as pcaps, PoCs and test results.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/ARPSyndicate/vedas-signatures/security/advisories/new) titled "Code of Conduct", or with GitHub's *Report content* feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
